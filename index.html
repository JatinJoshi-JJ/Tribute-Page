ORIGIN & FOUNDATIONS

The Journey

1971 — 1994

Early Life & Education

Born on June 28, 1971, in Pretoria, South Africa, Elon Reeve Musk exhibited an early fascination with computing and science. At age 12, he coded and sold the space-themed video game Blastar for approximately $500.

He later emigrated to Canada to attend Queen's University, subsequently transferring to the University of Pennsylvania, earning dual degrees in Physics and Economics.

1995 — 2002

Digital Frontier & PayPal

In 1995, Musk co-founded Zip2, an early online city guide with interactive vector maps, acquired by Compaq in 1999 for $307 million.

He immediately channeled his proceeds into founding X.com, an online financial services company that merged to become PayPal, acquired by eBay in 2002 for $1.5 billion.

2002 — 2012

SpaceX & Electric Revolution

Motivated by the belief that humanity must become a multiplanetary species to ensure survival, Musk founded SpaceX in 2002 to radically decrease space transportation costs.

In 2004, he led Tesla's Series A financing round, becoming chairman and product architect before stepping in as CEO in 2008 to steer mass electric vehicle production.

2016 — PRESENT

AI, Connectivity & Neural Systems

Musk broadened his vision to address civilizational bottlenecks: global satellite internet via Starlink, brain-computer interfaces with Neuralink, urban tunneling via The Boring Company, and frontier AI research with xAI.

His engineering philosophy is rooted in first-principles physics—breaking problems down to fundamental truths rather than reasoning by analogy.
